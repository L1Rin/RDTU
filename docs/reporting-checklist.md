# Historical reporting checklist

[Home](../README.md) · [Coverage map](coverage.md)

These are the author responses from the original NeurIPS manuscript checklist, preserved as historical reporting statements. Conference instructions and template guidelines are omitted. The original code-release answers refer to the manuscript's stated plans; this repository currently distributes supplementary text, tables, figures and document sources.

## Claims

Do the main claims made in the abstract and introduction accurately reflect the paper's contributions and scope?

**Original answer:** Yes

**Original justification:** Claims made in the abstract and introduction accurately reflect the paper's contributions and scope.

## Limitations

Does the paper discuss the limitations of the work performed by the authors?

**Original answer:** Yes

**Original justification:** We discuss limitations.

## Theory assumptions and proofs

**Original answer:** NA

**Original justification:** The paper does not present theoretical results.

## Experimental result reproducibility

**Original answer:** Yes

**Original justification:** We fully disclose all the information needed.
